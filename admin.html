Free trial plan

Every account — whether someone signs up on the site or you create it here — gets the same free credit, spent across a 5-hour session, the day, the week and the whole trial; every window allows at least one search while the trial has room. Visitors without an account share one small daily allowance. Saving with "start a new period" resets everyone's counters.
