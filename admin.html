Trial plan

Splits a credit budget between trial accounts and a small shared pool for visitors without an account. Each account's share is spent across a 5-hour session, the day, the week and the whole trial; every window allows at least one search while the trial has room. Saving with "start a new period" resets everyone's counters.

Trial accounts

Passwords are shown only once, right after they are created or reset. Download them and send each person their own username and password. The trial starts at the person's first sign-in.
